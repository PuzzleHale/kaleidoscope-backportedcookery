Asset attribution for Kaleidoscope: Backported Cookery
=======================================================

The following resource files are derived from the assets of
"Kaleidoscope Cookery / 森罗物语：厨房" and its unofficial Fabric continuation
"Kaleidoscope Cookery Refabricated / 森罗物语：厨房——重织" (MC 26.3, 1.6.0.1).
Original art authors: 药水棒冰 and the Kaleidoscope Official Production Team.

Item textures
  assets/kaleidoscope_backportedcookery/textures/item/*.png
  (copper_kitchen_knife, raw/cooked_donkey_meat, the rice bowls including
   delicious_egg_fried_rice, country-style vegetables, soups, braised_fish,
   yakitori, fruit_platter and shelf_mushroom_pot_soup)

Block textures
  assets/kaleidoscope_backportedcookery/textures/block/straw_bed.png
  assets/kaleidoscope_backportedcookery/textures/block/straw_bed_particle.png
  assets/kaleidoscope_backportedcookery/textures/block/shelf_mushroom_pot_soup.png

Block / item models and block states
  assets/kaleidoscope_backportedcookery/models/block/straw_bed_head.json
  assets/kaleidoscope_backportedcookery/models/block/straw_bed_foot.json
  assets/kaleidoscope_backportedcookery/models/block/straw_bed_full.json
  assets/kaleidoscope_backportedcookery/models/block/food/**/*.json
  assets/kaleidoscope_cookery/blockstates/*.json
  assets/kaleidoscope_cookery/models/item/*.json

  straw_bed_full.json is a merged derivative of the head and foot models
  (the foot half is rotated and translated to form one complete bed) used only
  by the item model. The shelf-mushroom pot-soup models were converted from the
  26.3 Blockbench format (multi-axis x/y/z Euler rotations) to the 1.21.1
  single-axis format; the geometry, UVs and textures are unchanged apart from
  that conversion.
